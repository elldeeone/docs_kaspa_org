## Overview

KasVault is a **browser extension wallet** for Kaspa that provides a user experience similar to MetaMask for Ethereum. It lives in your browser toolbar and allows you to send and receive KAS, manage multiple accounts, and connect to decentralized applications (dApps) directly from your browser.

KasVault is **open source** — the full source code is publicly available and can be audited by anyone. This transparency allows the community to verify that the wallet handles keys securely and does not include any malicious functionality.

<Callout type="info">
KasVault is a community-developed wallet. Always verify you are installing the official extension from the links below, and check the publisher/developer identity in your browser's extension store.
</Callout>

## Installation

KasVault is available as a browser extension for Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Brave, Edge) and Firefox.

### Chrome / Brave / Edge

1. Open the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com) and search for **KasVault**, or navigate directly to the KasVault listing.
2. Click **Add to Chrome** (or **Add to Brave** / **Add to Edge**).
3. Confirm the installation when prompted.
4. The KasVault icon will appear in your browser toolbar. You may need to click the extensions (puzzle piece) icon and pin KasVault for easy access.

### Firefox

1. Open [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org) and search for **KasVault**.
2. Click **Add to Firefox**.
3. Confirm the permissions and complete the installation.

### Building from Source

Since KasVault is open source, you can build it from the source code for maximum trust:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/aspect-build/kasvault.git
cd kasvault
npm install
npm run build
```

Then load the built extension as an unpacked extension in your browser's developer mode:

1. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/` (or the equivalent in your browser).
2. Enable **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `build` or `dist` directory from the cloned repository.

<Callout type="info">
Building from source ensures you are running exactly the code you have reviewed. However, you will need to manually update when new versions are released.
</Callout>

## Creating a New Wallet

1. Click the KasVault icon in your browser toolbar.
2. Select **Create New Wallet**.
3. Set a strong password. This password encrypts your wallet data in the browser's local storage. You will need it each time you unlock KasVault.
4. KasVault will generate a **12-word or 24-word mnemonic seed phrase**.
5. Write down the seed phrase on paper and store it in a secure location.
6. Confirm the seed phrase by entering the requested words.

<Callout>
Your seed phrase is the master key to all your accounts and funds. Anyone who obtains it can steal your KAS. Never share it, never store it digitally, and never enter it into any website or application other than KasVault itself or a trusted compatible wallet.
</Callout>

## Importing an Existing Wallet

If you already have a Kaspa wallet and want to use it in KasVault:

1. Click the KasVault icon and select **Import Wallet**.
2. Enter your existing mnemonic seed phrase (12 or 24 words).
3. Set a password for the KasVault extension.
4. KasVault will derive your addresses and display your balance.

You can also import a wallet using a **private key** for a single account (rather than a full mnemonic), which gives you access to one specific address without the full HD derivation tree.

## Managing Multiple Accounts

KasVault supports multiple accounts within a single wallet, all derived from the same seed phrase:

1. Click the account icon or dropdown at the top of the KasVault popup.
2. Select **Create Account** or **Add Account**.
3. The new account is automatically derived from your seed phrase using the next index in the BIP-32 derivation path.

Each account has its own set of addresses and maintains a separate balance display, but all accounts share the same underlying seed phrase. This is useful for separating funds by purpose (e.g., personal spending, project funds, mining income) while maintaining a single backup.

## Sending KAS

1. Click the KasVault icon to open the popup.
2. Click **Send**.
3. Enter the recipient's Kaspa address (`kaspa:` prefix).
4. Enter the amount of KAS to send.
5. Review the transaction details, including the estimated fee.
6. Click **Confirm** to sign and broadcast the transaction.

KasVault automatically handles UTXO selection and change address management. If your UTXOs need compounding before a large transaction, KasVault will handle the compounding process automatically or prompt you to initiate it.

## Receiving KAS

1. Click the KasVault icon to open the popup.
2. Click **Receive** or tap on your address.
3. Copy your Kaspa address or display the QR code.
4. Share the address with the sender.

## Connecting to dApps

One of KasVault's primary features is the ability to connect to Kaspa-based decentralized applications directly from your browser. When a dApp requests a wallet connection:

1. KasVault will display a **connection request popup** showing the dApp's origin URL.
2. Review the permissions the dApp is requesting.
3. Click **Connect** to authorize the connection.

Once connected, the dApp can:

- Read your account address (to display your balance or identify you).
- Request transaction signatures (you must approve each one individually in KasVault).

<Callout>
KasVault will always prompt you before signing any transaction or message. A connected dApp **cannot** spend your funds without your explicit approval in the KasVault popup. Always review the transaction details in the KasVault popup before approving.
</Callout>

### Managing Connected Sites

To review or revoke dApp connections:

1. Open KasVault and navigate to **Settings** > **Connected Sites**.
2. View the list of dApps that have been granted access.
3. Click **Disconnect** next to any dApp you want to revoke access from.

Regularly review your connected sites and disconnect any you no longer use.

## Network Selection

KasVault supports connecting to different Kaspa networks:

- **Mainnet** — The live Kaspa network with real KAS.
- **Testnet** — A test network for development and experimentation with test KAS.

You can switch networks in **Settings** > **Network**. This is primarily useful for developers who are testing dApps on testnet before deploying to mainnet.

## Security Considerations

### Extension Permissions

KasVault requires certain browser permissions to function (e.g., storage for encrypted wallet data, access to communicate with Kaspa nodes). Review the permissions listed in the extension store before installing.

### Password vs. Seed Phrase

- Your **password** encrypts the wallet data in local browser storage. It protects your wallet if someone accesses your browser.
- Your **seed phrase** is the ultimate backup. If you clear your browser data, reinstall the extension, or switch browsers, you will need the seed phrase to restore your wallet.

### Safe Browsing Habits

- Be cautious of phishing sites that mimic legitimate Kaspa dApps. Always verify the URL in the connection request popup.
- Do not install browser extensions from untrusted sources alongside KasVault — malicious extensions may attempt to intercept wallet data.
- Lock KasVault when you step away from your computer (it auto-locks after a configurable timeout).

<Callout>
For large holdings, consider using a [hardware wallet](/docs/wallets-and-tools/hardware-wallets) rather than a browser extension. KasVault is best suited for day-to-day transactions and dApp interactions where convenience is important.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

### Wallet Not Showing Balance

- Ensure you are connected to the correct network (mainnet vs. testnet).
- Check that the extension has an active connection to a Kaspa node (visible in Settings).
- Try refreshing the connection or restarting the extension.

### dApp Not Detecting KasVault

- Confirm the dApp supports KasVault as a wallet provider.
- Try refreshing the dApp page after installing or unlocking KasVault.
- Check that no other Kaspa wallet extensions are conflicting.

### Transaction Stuck

Kaspa transactions confirm in approximately 10 seconds under normal conditions. If a transaction appears stuck:

- Verify you are connected to a healthy node.
- Check the transaction ID on a Kaspa block explorer to see if it has actually been confirmed.
- The wallet display may need a refresh.
